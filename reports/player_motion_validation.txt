Player motion integration — 2026-11-13

Runtime changes
- All four player rigs receive a post-animation SkeletonModifier3D.
- Two-bone arm IK preserves segment lengths and wrist orientation.
- Shared tube/palm displacement provides recoil and equipment transitions.
- Pickup reach, held tomato, charged throw, eating and visor wiping drive hands.
- Authored locomotion is retained with ground-contact IK and upper-body motion.
- Incapacitated states defer to existing authored animation.
- First-person sleeves, forearms and fingers follow the visual weapon and item actions.
  These use a shared runtime mesh rather than each character's original skinned arms.
- Motion event serials and age are replicated; duplicate and stale events do not restart.
- Throw charge is replicated separately from authoritative gameplay.

Validation
PROCEDURAL_MOTION_OK: all four rigs, reachable hand target, segment length, finite poses.
CHARACTER_SWITCH_OK: four-character switching and existing sockets/gauges.
HEALING_TOMATO_OK: pickup, inventory, use and thrown damage regression.
PLAYER_MOTION_REPLICATION_OK: pickup/throw/eat decoding and duplicate suppression.
MAYO_NETWORK_OK: real local ENet host/client session and existing gameplay regression.
Rendered and inspected four character views and first-person view.

Limits
Ground adaptation augments existing locomotion rather than replacing it with a fully
procedural gait. No voice-driven lip sync is added because no player voice-sample
input was identified in this integration. Local-network checks do not simulate WAN
latency or packet loss. Existing item regression reports shutdown resource warnings.
